# The first plate

Three images, three different pixel sizes, and no cover image declared
anywhere. A reader that pages an archive by its rasters will make three pages
of this book and give each one the size of a picture; a reader that reads the
spine will make as many pages as the text needs.

![Plate A, sixty by forty](figures/plate-a.png)

The two are not close, and neither of them warns.

# The second plate

![Plate B, eighty by fifty](figures/plate-b.png)

A caption is a paragraph, not a picture, so a page built out of the picture
loses it.

# The third plate

![Plate C, forty by ninety](figures/plate-c.png)

The three plates are in reading order here and in the manifest. Nothing
guarantees they are in that order in the archive, and nothing about an archive
says which of them, if any, is the cover.
